What's new in v2.7.9

Installer is now in Bulgarian, with the right icon

The dialogs that walk you through the first install on a new Mac are now written in Bulgarian, and the icon shown in the dialogs is the familiar Аб app icon instead of the generic AppleScript scroll that macOS was defaulting to.

(This only affects the first-install DMG — once installed, LangAutoSwitcher itself is exactly the same as v2.7.8.)
